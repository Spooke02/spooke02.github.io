FAQ

What is the threshold?

For most states the aggregate-turnover limit for suppliers of services is Rs 20 lakh per financial year, and Rs 10 lakh in special-category states. Always check the current notification.

Do foreign payments count?

Income from clients abroad generally counts toward turnover. Export of services is usually zero-rated, often supported by an annual Letter of Undertaking, but the details depend on your case. Ask a CA.

Which exchange rate should I use?

Use the rate you actually received when the money landed, and keep the payment advice (e-FIRA) for each foreign credit.
