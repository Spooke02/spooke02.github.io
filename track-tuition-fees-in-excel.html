How to track tuition and coaching fees in Excel

A simple three-sheet setup that shows who has paid, who is pending and how regular each student is.

1. Three sheets

  • Students: ID, name, class, batch, monthly fee, months enrolled.
  • Payments: date, student ID, month paid for, amount, mode (cash or UPI).
  • Attendance: student ID and one column per day with P or A.

2. Work out what is due

Students columns: A ID, B name, C class, D batch, E monthly fee, F months enrolled. Payments columns: A date, B student ID, C month paid for, D amount. Add three columns to Students (G, H, I): total due is fee times months, paid is a sum from Payments, balance is the difference. For row 2:

G2 Total due:  =E2*F2
H2 Paid:       =SUMIFS(Payments!$D:$D, Payments!$B:$B, A2)
I2 Balance:    =G2-H2

Apply a red font to any balance above zero with conditional formatting, so pending fees stand out.

3. Attendance percentage

Present days:  =COUNTIF(C2:AG2,"P")
Marked days:   =COUNTIF(C2:AG2,"P")+COUNTIF(C2:AG2,"A")
Attendance %:  =IF(marked=0,"",present/marked)

4. Habits that keep it accurate

  • Use one student ID everywhere. Names are easy to mistype; IDs are not.
  • Log every payment the same day, including part payments.
  • Update months enrolled at the start of each month.
  • Keep the file private; it contains personal details.
