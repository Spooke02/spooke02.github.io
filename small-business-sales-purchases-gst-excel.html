Track sales, purchases and GST in Excel

A simple monthly view of what you sold, what you bought, the GST on each and a rough profit.

1. Three sheets

  • Sales: date, invoice no, customer, taxable value, GST rate %.
  • Purchases: date, bill no, supplier, taxable value, GST rate %.
  • Expenses: date, category, amount (costs without GST credit).

2. GST and totals per row

Columns on Sales and Purchases: A date, B number, C name, D taxable value, E GST rate %, then F and G are formulas.

F2 GST amount:  =D2*E2/100
G2 Total:       =D2+F2

3. A monthly summary

Put the year in B3 and the month number in B4, then:

Sales (taxable):  =SUMIFS(Sales!D:D, Sales!A:A, ">="&DATE(B3,B4,1), Sales!A:A, "<"&EDATE(DATE(B3,B4,1),1))
GST on sales:     =SUMIFS(Sales!F:F, ...same dates...)
GST on purchases: =SUMIFS(Purchases!F:F, ...same dates...)
Net GST estimate: = GST on sales - GST on purchases

Important: that net figure is a planning estimate. Your actual GST liability depends on your registration, the items you sell and credit rules. Confirm with a Chartered Accountant.

4. Simple profit

Profit = Sales (taxable) - Purchases (taxable) - Other expenses
