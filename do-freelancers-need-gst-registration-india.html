Do freelancers in India need GST registration?

Short answer: it depends on your turnover and your situation. Here is how to think about it, without the jargon.

The usual starting point: the threshold

GST registration is generally required once your aggregate turnover in a financial year crosses a threshold. For suppliers of services, sources commonly cite Rs 20 lakh for most states and Rs 10 lakh for special-category states. Check the current notification, because limits and conditions can change. You can test your numbers with the GST threshold calculator.

Do payments from clients abroad count?

Income from foreign clients is generally part of your turnover. Export of services is usually treated as zero-rated, often supported by an annual Letter of Undertaking (LUT), but the conditions matter (for example, how and in what currency you receive the money). Keep the paperwork for each foreign payment; see the e-FIRA/FIRC guide.

Can you register even if you are below the threshold?

Voluntary registration is possible in many cases, but it brings filing duties. Some clients also ask for a GSTIN. Whether it is worth it depends on your clients and costs, so ask a CA before you decide.

What to do in practice

  • Track every payment with date, client, currency, amount and the rupee amount credited.
  • Watch your running total against the threshold each month.
  • Keep each foreign payment's e-FIRA or FIRC.
  • Talk to a Chartered Accountant before you cross about 80% of the limit.

Questions to ask your CA

  • Does my kind of work qualify as export of services?
  • Do I need a LUT, and when do I file it?
  • How should my platform withdrawals and fees be recorded?
  • Should I register voluntarily?
