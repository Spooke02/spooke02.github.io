Udhar khata in Excel: track customer credit for your shop

A clean way to see who owes you, how much, and who is overdue, without a paper notebook.

1. Two sheets

  • Customers: customer ID, name, optional phone.
  • Transactions: date, customer ID, type (Credit or Payment), amount, note.

2. Balances by formula

Customers columns: A ID, B name, C phone. Transactions columns: A date, B customer ID, C type, D amount. Add D to I to Customers. For row 2:

D2 Total credit:  =SUMIFS(Transactions!$D:$D, Transactions!$B:$B, A2, Transactions!$C:$C, "Credit")
E2 Total paid:    =SUMIFS(Transactions!$D:$D, Transactions!$B:$B, A2, Transactions!$C:$C, "Payment")
F2 Balance:       =D2-E2

3. Last credit date and overdue

G2 Last credit:   =MAXIFS(Transactions!$A:$A, Transactions!$B:$B, A2, Transactions!$C:$C, "Credit")
H2 Days since:    =TODAY()-G2
I2 Overdue?:      =IF(AND(F2>0, H2>30), "Overdue", IF(F2>0, "Owes", "Clear"))

MAXIFS needs Excel 2019 or newer, or Google Sheets. Change 30 to the number of days you allow.

4. Good habits

  • Write every credit and every payment as a separate row, never edit old rows.
  • Agree the credit limit and due date with the customer when you give credit.
  • Keep customer details private.
