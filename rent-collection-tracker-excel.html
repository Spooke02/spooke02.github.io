Rent collection tracker in Excel

See at a glance which units have paid and which still owe rent.

1. Two sheets

  • Units columns: A unit ID, B tenant, C monthly rent, D first rent month, E deposit (then F to I are formulas).
  • Payments columns: A date received, B unit ID, C amount.

2. Months due, rent due and balance

For row 2 of the Units sheet:

F2 Months due:     =MAX(0,(YEAR(TODAY())-YEAR(D2))*12+MONTH(TODAY())-MONTH(D2)+1)
G2 Rent due:       =C2*F2
H2 Rent received:  =SUMIFS(Payments!$C:$C, Payments!$B:$B, A2)
I2 Balance:        =G2-H2

A positive balance is rent pending; a negative one means the tenant has paid in advance.

3. Tips

  • Count the start month as a due month, or change the formula if you bill from the next month.
  • Record every payment with the date it actually arrived.
  • Keep the deposit in its own column and do not mix it with rent.
  • Keep tenant details private.
